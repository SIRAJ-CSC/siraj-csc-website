Early Stage · R&D Initiative

Technology & Automation

We are exploring AI-assisted automation tools for digital service and document workflows — a separate research initiative built around our experience as a CSC operator.

Development Status: Early Stage
No finished software product is currently available. This initiative is completely separate from SIRAJ CSC's core service centre operations, which continue to run normally.
What We Are Exploring

AI-Assisted Automation for Digital Service Workflows

CSC operators and digital service providers routinely perform repetitive browser-based tasks — navigating government portals, filling forms, uploading documents, checking status, and generating outputs. Much of this work is manual and time-consuming for operators at scale.

We are exploring whether AI language models and browser automation can assist with these workflows in a controlled way — reducing repetitive effort while keeping sensitive actions under human oversight.

Technology Areas Under Research

Large Language Models (LLMs)

Document understanding, reasoning and workflow intelligence using LLMs.

Browser Automation

Controlled, supervised navigation of service portals — no credential bypass or CAPTCHA circumvention.

Document Processing

OCR, vision models and structured data extraction from scanned documents.

Human-in-the-Loop

All sensitive actions require human review. No fully autonomous decision-making for government or financial tasks.

What This Is Not

  • CAPTCHA bypass software
  • Credential harvesting or account scraping tools
  • Unauthorized access to government systems
  • A finished or commercially available product
  • Affiliated with any government programme or ministry
