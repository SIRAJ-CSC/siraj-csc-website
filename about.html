Who We Are

SIRAJ CSC, Kalaburagi

SIRAJ CSC is an authorised Common Service Centre (CSC) operator located in Kalaburagi, Karnataka. We operate as part of the CSC e-Governance Services India Limited (CSC SPV) network — enabling citizens to access government and digital services at the local level.

Our centre provides a wide range of services — Aadhaar, PAN, banking, insurance, ticketing and government certificates — all under one roof, conveniently accessible to our local community.

Important: SIRAJ CSC is a private digital-service business operating as an authorised CSC operator. We are not a government department or agency. Services are provided through authorised CSC platforms.

CSC e-Governance Network

Part of the CSC Network

SIRAJ CSC operates as an authorised Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE) under CSC e-Governance Services India Limited (CSC SPV) — a Special Purpose Vehicle set up by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India.

The CSC network enables citizens to access government services, digital platforms and citizen-centric services through authorised operators at the local level.

Clarification: SIRAJ CSC is a private digital-service business. We are not a government department. We operate as an authorised CSC operator and facilitate access to various government and digital platforms. Always verify service requirements and fees before proceeding.
