Coverage Options

Insurance Coverage Types

We assist with both mandatory third party and comprehensive insurance options.

Mandatory by Law

Third Party Insurance

Mandatory for all vehicles under Indian Motor Vehicles Act. Covers damage or injury caused to a third party. Minimum legal requirement for every vehicle owner on Indian roads.

Recommended

Comprehensive Insurance

Covers third party liability AND damage to your own vehicle — from accidents, theft, fire, natural calamities and other risks. Strongly recommended for all vehicle owners for complete protection.
