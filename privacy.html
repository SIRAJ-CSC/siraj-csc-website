Privacy Policy

How we handle information on this website.

Last updated: October 2026

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the SIRAJ CSC website at sirajcsc.com (the "Website"). It explains how we handle any information that may be collected when you visit this Website.

SIRAJ CSC is an early-stage technology initiative based in Kalaburagi, Karnataka, India. This Website is an informational presence for our business and ongoing development work.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

This Website does not currently:

  • Require you to create an account or register
  • Collect or store any personal data through forms
  • Use advertising networks or tracking pixels
  • Sell or share personal data with third parties for commercial purposes
  • Process payments or financial information

3. Information That May Be Collected Automatically

Like most websites, this Website may collect standard technical information automatically through server logs and hosting infrastructure, including:

  • IP address (anonymised where possible)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and approximate time of visit
  • Referring URL

This information is used only for basic operational purposes (such as security monitoring) and is not used to personally identify individual visitors.

4. Third-Party Services

Google Fonts

This Website loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). When your browser loads these fonts, a request is made to Google's servers. Google's privacy practices apply to this request. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Hosting

The Website may be hosted on a third-party hosting platform. The hosting provider may collect standard access logs as part of their infrastructure. We do not control those logs beyond standard hosting agreements.

5. Email Contact

If you contact us by email at info@sirajcsc.com, we will receive the content of your email and your email address. We will use this information only to respond to your enquiry. We do not add you to any mailing lists without your explicit consent.

6. Cookies

This Website does not currently set any cookies of its own. Third-party services (such as Google Fonts) may set their own cookies subject to their own policies.

7. Children's Privacy

This Website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the Website and our activities evolve. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when changes are made.
